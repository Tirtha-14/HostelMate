HostelMate – Smart Roommate Matching System

HostelMate helps hostel students find compatible roommates based on their preferences, habits, and lifestyle. Students register their sleeping schedule, study habits, cleanliness, noise tolerance, social preference, and more. A weighted compatibility engine scores every possible pair, and a greedy allocation algorithm groups students into 2 / 3 / 4-sharing rooms — ensuring each student is placed exactly once, in the best-matching group available.

Register a Student Run Room Allocation

How it works

  1. Register — every student fills out a preference profile.
  2. Score — HostelMate computes a 0–100 compatibility score for every pair using weighted attribute matching.
  3. Allocate — a greedy nearest-neighbour algorithm groups students into rooms of their preferred size, maximizing average in-room compatibility.
