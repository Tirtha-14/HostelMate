HostelMate – Smart Roommate Matching System

HostelMate helps students find compatible roommates based on their lifestyle preferences, habits, and room-sharing requirements.

Students provide information such as sleeping schedule, study habits, cleanliness, noise tolerance, social preference, guest frequency, food preference, and preferred roommates.

How HostelMate Works

  1. Register — Students create an account and complete their preference profile.
  2. Score — HostelMate calculates compatibility scores between students using weighted lifestyle preferences.
  3. Optimize — The system searches possible room combinations and prioritizes placing the maximum number of students into complete rooms.
  4. Match — When the number of students placed is the same, the allocation with the higher total compatibility is selected.
  5. Finalize — An administrator reviews the generated allocation and locks it.
